# The values' notes

What `prototypes/solve-or-boom/config.js` said beside its values, kept here because `data/config.json`
holds no comments. The values themselves are in `data/config.json`.

- `seed`: every gameplay random call reads rng(), seeded from this (or ?seed= in the address)
- `default_map`: the map the session screen starts on (pequeno, grande, patio, pasillos, dospisos: data/maps/<id>.json); C or a click changes it there

---- Player -------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `mouse_sens`: multiplier; [ and ] on the pause screen
- `key_turn_speed`: radians/s when turning with the arrow keys
- `run_speed`: m/s, the normal pace (no key held)
- `walk_speed`: m/s holding the walk key (X by default; keys in data/keybinds.json)
- `sprint_speed`: m/s holding the sprint key (Left Shift) while moving; no shooting while sprinting; it stands you up from prone
- `crouch_eye`: m, eye height while holding crouch (C); standing is EYE, 1.6 m, in src/game/map.ts
- `crouch_speed_mult`: speed multiplier while crouched
- `crouch_spread_mult`: your shot spread multiplier while crouched
- `crouch_hit_mult`: bots' hit chance against you, multiplied, while crouched
- `prone_eye`: m, eye height while prone (Z toggles)
- `prone_speed_mult`: speed multiplier while prone
- `prone_spread_mult`: your shot spread multiplier while prone
- `prone_hit_mult`: bots' hit chance against you, multiplied, while prone
- `prone_time`: s to get down or up; no moving or shooting meanwhile; the stance counts from the end of it
- `jump_velocity`: m/s upward at take-off (Space); only standing, on the ground
- `gravity`: m/s² pulling a jump back down
- `air_spread_mult`: your shot spread multiplier in the air
- `eye_rate`: how fast the eye moves to a new stance's height (share of the gap closed per second; higher is snappier)
- `armor_absorb`: share of damage armour takes
- `headshot_mult`: the player's shots only

---- Rounds -------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `results_countdown_s`: s; single player's results screen counts down this long (on the game's fixed step, only while the results show) and then starts the next match with the chosen mode and map (30)
- `buy_time`: s; gates closed, buying open. The default of a match (35); the session screen steps it by `step` between `min` and `max`, and a teacher's choice is clamped to that range
- round time: s until defenders win by time; each map has its own, `round_time` in data/maps/<map>.json (Pequeño 105, Grande 150, Patio 140, Pasillos 180, Dos pisos 150)
- `bomb_time`: s from plant to boom
- `plant_time`: s holding E on a site
- `defuse_answers`: right answers to cut the bomb
- `wrong_delay`: s the card shakes before the new question
- `halftime_after`: rounds before the sides swap
- `round_end_delay`: s the round banner stays

---- Economy ($) --------------------------------------------------------------------
- `loss_bonus`: extra per loss in a row

---- Weapons: damage per hit, seconds between shots, magazine, reserve, spread (radians)
- `sniper_spread`: not aiming; aimed is sniper_aim_spread
- `move_spread`: added while running (story 015: 0.035 to 0.008, so moving adds a modest amount: a rifle on the move is about twice as wide as standing)
- `muzzle_fwd` 0.6 m, `muzzle_side` 0.18 m, `muzzle_down` 0.18 m: where an unaimed shot and its tracer leave the player, from the eye: ahead, to the right, below (the gun in the hand). Pulled back toward the eye when a wall or crate is in the way (story 015)
- `sights_down` 0.08 m: where an aimed shot and its tracer leave: on the centre line, `muzzle_fwd` ahead and this far below the eye (the sights, not the shoulder)
- Story 015 spread at 20 m (radius of the group, standing / moving / aimed): pistol 0.009 (0.25 m) / 0.017 / 0.003; smg 0.013 (0.37 m) / 0.021 / 0.005; rifle 0.007 (0.20 m) / 0.015 / 0.002; sniper 0.04 (1.1 m) / 0.048 / 0.0005 (0.014 m). Before: 0.012 / 0.02 / 0.01 / 0.07 standing, +0.035 moving, aimed 0.004 / 0.008 / 0.003 / 0.002
- `pistol_aim_fov`: view angle in degrees while aiming with this gun (72 is the normal view; smaller zooms more); mouse turn scales with it
- `pistol_aim_spread`: shot spread in radians while aiming (replaces the gun spread and move_spread)
- `pistol_aim_speed`: walk speed multiplier while aiming
- `smg_aim_fov`: view angle in degrees while aiming with this gun (72 is the normal view; smaller zooms more); mouse turn scales with it
- `smg_aim_spread`: shot spread in radians while aiming (replaces the gun spread and move_spread)
- `smg_aim_speed`: walk speed multiplier while aiming
- `rifle_aim_fov`: view angle in degrees while aiming with this gun (72 is the normal view; smaller zooms more); mouse turn scales with it
- `rifle_aim_spread`: shot spread in radians while aiming (replaces the gun spread and move_spread)
- `rifle_aim_speed`: walk speed multiplier while aiming
- `sniper_aim_fov`: view angle in degrees while aiming with this gun (72 is the normal view; smaller zooms more); mouse turn scales with it
- `sniper_aim_spread`: shot spread in radians while aiming (replaces the gun spread and move_spread)
- `sniper_aim_speed`: walk speed multiplier while aiming

---- Bots: the challenge ramps from round 1 to round 1 + ramp_rounds ------------------
- `bot_react_start`: s before a bot fires at a new target
- `bot_acc_start`: base hit chance per shot
- `bot_facil_react_mult`: bot level Fácil: multiplies `bot_react_start/end` (a bot's reaction time); above 1 is slower
- `bot_facil_acc_mult`: bot level Fácil: multiplies `bot_acc_start/end` (a bot's hit chance)
- `bot_normal_react_mult`: bot level Normal: multiplies `bot_react_start/end` (a bot's reaction time); 1 is the game as before
- `bot_normal_acc_mult`: bot level Normal: multiplies `bot_acc_start/end` (a bot's hit chance); 1 is the game as before
- `bot_dificil_react_mult`: bot level Difícil: multiplies `bot_react_start/end` (a bot's reaction time); above 1 is slower
- `bot_dificil_acc_mult`: bot level Difícil: multiplies `bot_acc_start/end` (a bot's hit chance)
- `bot_fire_slow`: bots fire this much slower than the gun allows
- `bot_speed_rusher`: m/s
- `bot_view_range`: m
- `bot_answer_right`: chance a bot answers a buy question
- `bot_defuse_right`: chance a bot's defuse answer is right
- `bot_think_min`: s a bot thinks per defuse question
- `sniper_hold_time`: s an attacking sniper watches mid first
- `bot_cover_hp`: a bot below this health that sees an enemy goes to cover (being hit, or reloading with an enemy about, always does)
- `bot_cover_time`: s a bot stays in cover (peeking and hiding) before it takes up its plan again; it never leaves mid-reload
- `bot_cover_search`: cells (walking steps) a bot looks around itself for a cover cell: floor beside a wall or crate with no line of sight to the threat
- `bot_cover_retry`: s before a bot that found no cover looks again
- `bot_peek_show`, `bot_peek_hide`: s out at the peek cell (where it can shoot) and s back behind cover, in turn
- `bot_threat_memory`: s an enemy last seen still counts as "about" for cover while reloading
- `bot_group_wait`: s attackers wait at the staging point for the rest, and rotating defenders wait for the ones behind, before going anyway
- `bot_group_gap`: m a rotating defender lets the farthest teammate fall behind before it waits for them
- `bot_buddy_range`: m: a bot that loses sight of its target goes to where it was last seen, and the nearest free teammate this close goes too
- `bot_site_alert`: m from a site an enemy seen or heard by a defender calls the defenders to rotate there
- `bot_hear_shot`: m a gunshot is heard (was a fixed 24)
- `bot_hear_sprint`, `bot_hear_run`: m your footsteps are heard sprinting (Shift) or running; walking (X), crouched or prone is silent
- `bot_hear_land`: m the landing of your jump is heard
- `bot_hear_floor_mult`: maps with heights: the part of a noise's range a bot on the other level hears, when no clear line (an opening, the ramp) joins the two
- `bot_step_interval`: s between your footstep noises
- `bot_corner_look`: s an attacker looks at each entry of a site on arriving there
- `bot_corner_merge_deg`: entries closer than this angle, seen from the bot, count as one look
- `bot_sep_dist`: m bots keep from any other body (they spread out instead of walking in single file)
- `bot_stuck_give_up`: maps with heights: how many stuck re-paths in a row (each after 0.8 s without moving) before a bot gives its current waypoint up
- `bot_repath_strict`: s a bot that got stuck walks its new path cell centre by cell centre; stuck again within it, it snaps to the cell centre

---- Question bank ------------------------------------------------------------------
- `wrong_delay_growth`: factor the lockout grows by for each wrong answer in a row (2 doubles it, 1 keeps it flat)
- `question_step_<item>` (smg, rifle, sniper, armor, ammo): levels above the session level that the item's buy question asks (0 = the same level, capped at Difícil; the defuse uses the session level; a custom CSV ignores it)
- `wrong_delay_max`: s cap of the wrong-answer lockout; `wrong_delay` grows by `wrong_delay_growth` for each wrong answer in a row, a right answer resets it
- `retry_gap`: draws between a missed question and its one retry (a pool smaller than this brings it back after one other question)
- `retry_max`: most times one missed question comes back in a match
- Math ranges per level (facil, medio, dificil): medio keeps the ranges the game had before levels; a custom CSV ignores the level
- `math_arith_a_min_<level>`, `math_arith_a_max_<level>`: first operand of sums and differences (inclusive)
- `math_arith_b_min_<level>`, `math_arith_b_max_<level>`: second operand of sums and differences (inclusive)
- `math_mult_min_<level>`, `math_mult_max_<level>`: both factors of a multiplication or a division
- `math_frac_den_min_<level>`, `math_frac_den_max_<level>`: denominators (min 3); difícil adds fractions with different denominators
- `math_frac_mult_max_<level>`: "n/d of N" uses N = d x (2 to this)
- `math_pct_p_min_<level>`, `math_pct_p_max_<level>`: the percentage, in steps of 5
- `math_pct_base_min_<level>`, `math_pct_base_max_<level>`: the number it is taken of, in steps of 20 so the answer is whole
- `math_eq_x_max_<level>`: the answer x is 1 to this
- `math_eq_a_max_<level>`: the multiplier a is 2 to this
- `math_eq_b_max_<level>`: the added number b is 1 to this; difícil adds a(x + b) = c and ax + b = cx + d

- `sfx_limit_db`: the limiter's threshold in dB on everything heard; lower squeezes overlapping fire harder (no clipping either way)
- `sfx_<gun>_level`: each gun's shot loudness (pistol, smg, rifle, sniper), before the volume and a bot's distance
- `sfx_<gun>_tail`: seconds of each gun's room tail; the number of echoes is tail / sfx_echo_delay, at most 4 (sniper longest)
- `sfx_click_mix`, `sfx_thump_mix`: the sharp crack and the low thump in every shot, against the noise body (1 = as designed)
- `sfx_echo_delay`, `sfx_echo_gain`: seconds between echoes, and how much quieter each echo is than the one before
- `sfx_pan_strength`: how far left or right a bot's shot sits from its direction to the camera (0 = centred, 1 = full)
- `sfx_near_cutoff`, `sfx_far_cutoff`, `sfx_far_dist`: a bot's shot is low-passed from near_cutoff Hz (beside you) to far_cutoff Hz at far_dist metres and beyond; your own gun is never dulled
- `sfx_reload_level`, `sfx_empty_level`: the reload (mag out, mag in, slide or bolt, spread over reload_time) and the dry trigger click

---- Classroom server (data/aula.json; npm run aula) -------------------------------
- `port`: the port students open; the AULA_PORT variable or --puerto N overrides it
- `port_tries`: when the port is busy, how many next ports to try before giving up
- `demo_rooms`: bots-only rooms `npm run aula` starts so there is something to see; --salas N overrides (0 = none)
- `tick_ms`: ms between the server's timer ticks; each tick runs the fixed steps that are due
- `tick_budget_ms`: most ms one tick may spend stepping rooms; the rooms left over wait for the next tick, so one slow room cannot starve the others
- `max_catchup_steps`: most fixed steps one room runs in one tick (it catches up after a late tick)
- `max_backlog_steps`: a room more than this many steps behind drops the rest (counted and logged), instead of racing to catch up
- `empty_grace_s`: seconds a room that had human players keeps running after the last one leaves, before it is closed
- `rejoin_grace_s`: seconds a student's seat is held after their connection drops (a reload or a Wi-Fi drop); when it runs out the seat is a bot again (--gracia N overrides; 0 = at once)
- `rematch_vote_s`: seconds a teacher room's students see the results with the vote (next mode and map, Continuar) before the room starts the next match by itself; the server's clock counts it (story 029)
- `lobby_tick_ms`: ms between the lobby's checks (grace expiry, closed rooms); the grace is accurate to about this
- `max_connections`: connections beyond this are refused
- `max_message_bytes`: a message longer than this is dropped
- `max_messages_per_second`, `message_burst`: a connection's allowance (a token bucket); messages beyond it are dropped
- `max_strikes`: dropped or invalid messages a connection may send before the server closes it
- `max_name_length`: longest name a student can give (kept in memory only, never logged)
- `max_look_per_message`: radians, the most one input message may turn the aim in yaw or pitch; one over it is clamped to it (by the page before sending and by the server), never refused
- `ping_interval_s`: seconds between the server's pings; a connection that did not answer the last one is closed
- `log_repeat_s`: the same kind of log line is printed at most once per this many seconds
- `status_every_s`: seconds between the one-line room summary in the terminal (0 = never)
- `load_rooms`, `load_seconds`: the load check (npm run aula -- --carga): rooms of bots, and how long; --segundos N overrides
- `net_interp_ms`: classroom client: milliseconds in the past the other players are drawn, between two snapshots (two snapshots at snapshot_hz 20)
- `net_snap_m`: classroom client: a prediction error over this many metres is corrected at once instead of blended
- `net_correct_blend`: classroom client: the share of a smaller prediction error corrected at each snapshot (0.35 at 20 a second: about 100 ms to halve)
- `snapshot_hz` (aula.json): snapshots per second sent to each student; the 60-step rate divided by a whole number of steps
- `event_range_m` (aula.json): metres within which a student gets another player's shot (sound and tracer)
- `input_queue_max` (aula.json): a student's inputs waiting to be played, at most (8 = 133 ms); older ones are dropped
- `input_hold_steps` (aula.json): steps a student's held controls are repeated when no input arrived in time, before idling

## Teacher screen (story 006), data/aula.json
- `demo_rooms` 0: the server no longer starts running rooms of bots (they would take students before the teacher sets up). `--salas N` still makes N.
- `waiting_rooms` 4: empty waiting rooms made at start so arriving students have a seat (4 rooms = 32 students) until "Repartir automáticamente" replaces them. 0 = students get "sin cupo" until the teacher adds a room.
- `max_rooms` 16: most live rooms (the split and "add room" refuse past it). `finished_rooms_shown` 8: ended rooms still listed to the teacher.
- `teacher_room_name_max` 24: most letters in a room's name ("3.º A", "Grupo rojo"); the teacher's rename is trimmed, a blank one clears it, a longer one is refused in Spanish (story 027). The name lives on the server's room record until the room is removed.
- `teacher_update_ms` 500: how often the teacher page's state is checked and pushed if changed; `teacher_heartbeat_s` 5: pushed anyway at least this often.
- `teacher_max_message_bytes` 393216: largest teacher frame (a 262144-byte CSV is 349528 base64 chars). `csv_max_bytes` 262144 and `csv_max_questions` 500: the CSV limits (single player has none; these protect the server).

## Results (story 007), data/aula.json
- `results_max_students` 1000: names the results store holds at most (memory only, nothing on disk); a new name past it is not counted. A class is 30 to 200 students; this only stops a runaway client opening names forever.
- `weak_below_pct` 60: the teacher's topic view marks a topic "a reforzar" when fewer than this percent of its answers are right (60 = a bare pass in the usual 0 to 100 marking).
- `weak_min_answers` 5: ...and only once it has at least this many answers, so one early miss does not mark a topic.

## Weapons and effects (story 008), data/config.json
- `fx_<gun>_flash` (pistol .14, smg .17, rifle .24, sniper .34): size of the muzzle flash star (metres, in the first-person gun's space; other players' flashes use the same value, scaled to their model).
- `fx_<gun>_tracer_w` (story 015: .02 to .06, half of before) and `fx_<gun>_tracer_life` (.06 to .22 s): thickness of the tracer bar and how long it stays; a bigger gun leaves a thicker, longer-lived line. The bar always runs from the gun to the hit point.
- `fx_spark_*`: the pale burst on a wall, floor or crate: cubes thrown (`count`), their speed (m/s), size (m) and life (s). `fx_hit_*`: the same for the red burst on a player. `fx_gravity`: how fast both fall (m/s per s). Story 015: `fx_spark_size` 0.13 to 0.07 m.
- `fx_boom_fireball` 7 (see the second pass below) / `fx_boom_ring` 30 / `fx_boom_column` 26: the explosion's fireball radius, the shockwave ring radius and the fire column's height (metres); the column is what makes it readable over walls from across the map. `fx_boom_debris` 28 cubes at `fx_boom_debris_speed` 14 m/s.
- `fx_shake` .14 (metres of camera shake at the blast) and `fx_shake_range` 45 (m): the shake falls off with distance to the bomb, never below a fifth of it, and fades over the blast's 1.2 s. 0 turns the shake off.
- Story 008 second pass (the first was not readable on screen): `fx_spark_*` raised to 8 cubes, 4 m/s, 0.13 m, 0.45 s; `fx_hit_*` to 14 cubes, 4.5 m/s, 0.16 m, 0.6 s.
- `fx_spark_puff` 0.4 m (was 0.7, story 015) / `fx_spark_puff_life` 0.25 s: a bright pale puff where a shot meets a wall, floor or crate. `fx_hit_puff` 0.45 m / `fx_hit_puff_life` 0.25 s: the red puff on a player, drawn over the model so the body never hides it.
- `fx_hit_tint` 0.12 s: how long a hit player's team colour flashes red.
- `fx_mark_size` 0.25 m (was 0.5, story 015) / `fx_mark_life` 1.5 s: the dark mark a shot leaves on a wall, crate or floor (16 at most, the oldest reused); it fades over the second half of its life.
- `fx_boom_screen_time` 0.12 s / `fx_boom_screen_alpha` 0.45: the full-screen cream flash of the explosion, short and light so the fireball, ring and column show after it (it used to last the whole 1.2 s). `fx_boom_ball_time` 0.15 s: how long the white flash ball lasts.
- `fx_boom_fireball` lowered from 12 to 7 m in the second pass, so a viewer 8 m away sees the fireball and the ring from outside it instead of a screen filled with yellow.
- `chr_stride` 4 rad/m: how fast a character'"'"'s legs cycle per metre walked, so the swing speed follows the speed. `chr_leg_swing` 0.7 rad / `chr_arm_swing` 0.3 rad: the leg and arm swing at full walk. `chr_bob` 0.04 m: how much the body rises with each step. `chr_blend` 12 /s: how fast a pose (crouch, prone, walk or stand) eases in. `chr_death_time` 0.7 s: how long the fall to the floor takes (the body then stays down until the round resets).
- `scn_clouds` 14: how many blocky clouds float high round the arena (3 or 4 white blocks each, flat bottom, 210 to 260 m away, never in front of the arena). `scn_cloud_size` 16 m: the width of one cloud block. `scn_sun_size` 6 degrees: the radius of the sun disc. `scn_floor_wear` 0.6: soft worn patches on the floor, per floor cell (0 = clean). `scn_base_tint` 0.4: how far the walls round a base turn to its side's colour (0 none, 1 full). `scn_flag_height` 5.8 m: the height of the bomb-site and base flags (the walls are 4.5 m, so the flags show above them from across the arena).
- `scn_slab_thick` 0.25 m: how thick the first-floor slab and the ramps are drawn (story 011: a stand-in look so a test map with heights reads; story 012 draws the real first floor). Drawing only: the rules treat the slab as a plane at `level_height`.
- `scn_rail_height` 1.0 m: the low rail drawn along the first floor's open edges (story 012). Drawing only: the rules have no rails, so it stays under eye height (1.6 m) and you see and shoot over it.
- `scn_stair_steps_per_cell` 6: how many steps a ramp cell is drawn as (story 012; 6 gives 0.5 m treads and 0.25 m risers on a 2-cell stair). Drawing only: the rules walk the smooth slope.
- `level_height` 3 m: how high the first floor is above the ground (story 011; a map with a first floor or ramps only). The walls are 4.5 m and the eye 1.6 m, so a person on the first floor has the eye at 4.6 m, just over the wall tops. Under it there is 3 m of headroom for the 1.8 m body and a jump (0.83 m).
- `step_height` 0.5 m: the most a body steps up when walking (a ramp is climbed continuously; a floor higher than this is a wall to it). A drop of more than this is a fall: the body is set down on the lower floor with the landing noise (no damage).
- `ramp_max_slope` 0.6: the steepest a ramp or stair may be (rise over run); the map check names a steeper one. With level_height 3 a ramp must be 2 cells (6 m) long or more (slope 0.5).
- `bomb_reach_y` 1.5 m: how far apart in height a person and the bomb may be to pick it up or to open a defuse: a defender under a planted bomb on the floor above (3 m) cannot defuse through the ceiling.
- `touch_sens` 1: multiplier of the finger's turn on a phone or tablet (the pause screen's - and + change it, as for `mouse_sens`); `touch_turn` 0.005 rad per CSS pixel: how far one pixel of drag on the right half turns the view at `touch_sens` 1 (a swipe across the half is about 120 degrees; the turn shrinks with the zoom while aiming, like the mouse).
- `touch_stick_size` 130 px: the diameter of the left stick (CSS pixels); the knob moves half of it each way. `touch_deadzone` 0.3: the part of the stick's radius the knob must pass before it moves you (the move is 8-way, like the keys: the network protocol takes -1, 0 or 1 per axis).
- `touch_button_size` 56 px: the width of the round on-screen buttons (jump, crouch, reload, swap gun, buy, aim, pause, plant or defuse); fire is 1.4 times that. Thumb-sized: 44 px is the least Apple and Google advise.
- `touch_pixel_ratio` 1.5: the most the phone graphics level draws per CSS pixel (3D and the overlay), used on touch devices instead of the desktop's 2; a phone with a ratio of 3 would draw four times the pixels for no visible gain.
- `fx_scope_tracer_skip`: metres of the own sniper tracer left undrawn when seen through the scope (story 015): the 22° view magnifies the first metres into a white blob
- `fx_scope_tracer_w`: the own sniper tracer's width when seen through the scope (story 015)
- `CRATE_TOP` (src/game/map.ts, not config: the models' size): a player's shot passes over a crate cell above its drawn height, crate 2.2, barrel 2.62, pallet 2.12, sandbag 1.5 m (story 015)
- `first_kill_bonus` $500 (story 016): paid on top of `kill_money` to whoever makes the first kill of each round (a bot too); a person sees "Primera baja: +$500". Not paid for a bomb or self kill.
- `price_medkit` $400 (story 016): the buy menu's Botiquín; it is carried (at most `medkit_max`, 2) and used with its own key (G) or the touch button; it is lost on death and at half-time.
- `medkit_heal` 50 hp: what a used medkit gives, and what a medkit picked up from the floor gives (never over `max_hp`).
- `drop_ammo_mags` 1: a dead player drops an ammo box and one medkit. Walking over the box adds this many magazines to each gun the taker holds whose reserve is not full (up to its `<gun>_reserve`); walking over the medkit puts it in the pack while they carry fewer than `medkit_max`. A full pack and full reserves leave both on the floor.
- `pickup_radius` 1 m, `pickup_reach_y` 1.5 m: how close (flat distance, and feet height apart on a map with heights) a living person must be to take a dropped item by walking over it.
- `pickup_life` 30 s: a dropped item vanishes after this long, and always at the end of the round.
- `melee_dmg` 35, `melee_range` 1.6 m, `melee_rate` 0.6 s (story 016): with the magazine and reserve both empty, firing is a strike: a short ray like a shot's, no headshot bonus, quiet (bots do not hear it). `melee_time` 0.3 s is how long the swing animation lasts.
- `question_step_medkit` 0: the medkit's question is the match level.
- `touch_fire_scale`: the touch Fuego button's size as a multiple of touch_button_size (the big button 2 of the HUD layout)
- `touch_aim_scale`: the touch Apuntar button's size as a multiple of touch_button_size (button 3 of the HUD layout)
- `view_fov`: the normal view angle in degrees (the camera, and the base the aimed zoom's mouse turn is scaled against) (TD-003)
- `pickup_offset`: metres a dead player's medkit and ammo lie to each side; a side inside a wall or crate falls back to the spot itself (TD-001)
- `medkit_full_msg_s`: seconds before "Ya tienes toda la vida" can show again when the medkit key is pressed at full health (TD-002)
- `hit_radius`: a player's body radius for shots, in metres (the human's shot and the bots' friendly-fire line check) (TD-006)
- `ff_line_min`: with friendly fire, the nearest a teammate can stand in a bot's line and still take its shot, in metres (TD-006)
- `ff_line_dy`: with friendly fire, the largest height difference for a teammate to be in a bot's line, in metres (TD-006)
- `fx_vm_converge` 4: the first-person gun held from the hip is turned so its barrel meets the crosshair's line this far ahead (in the gun view's units); aimed, the sights sit on the crosshair whatever this is.
- `fx_vm_aim_back` 0.35: aimed, the first-person gun sits this much further from the eye than from the hip, so it covers less of the view around the crosshair.
- `medkit_time` 3 s: using a carried medkit takes this long (walking allowed, a bar shows it); the medkit is spent and the health given when it ends. Shooting, reloading, changing gun, opening the buy menu or a card, or dying stops it and keeps the medkit.

## Story 018: streak rewards (option "Premios por racha", on by default)
- `streak_shield_at` 3: right answers in a row (buy and defuse cards) that give the Escudo; a wrong answer sets the streak to 0, dying does not; bots never earn
- `streak_hunter_at` 5: the streak that gives the ability Cazador, kept (one at most: reaching it again with one kept keeps one) until used with its key (H) or touch button
- `streak_gold_at` 8: the streak that gives the Rifle dorado (replaces the primary, kept until death or the half-time swap); the streak then starts again at 0. Reaching it while holding one refills its ammo
- `streak_shield` 50: armour given at once by the Escudo, capped at 100 like bought armour
- `hunter_time` 10 s: how long the Cazador shows the enemies on the minimap and speeds you up; dying ends it
- `hunter_speed` 0.3: the Cazador's speed bonus (+30 %), on top of walk, run, sprint and stance speeds
- `gold_dmg` 45 (rifle 34), `gold_rate` 0.1 (rifle 0.11), `gold_mag` 30, `gold_reserve` 90: the Rifle dorado, based on the rifle's keys; it is never in the shop (bought ammo refills it like any held gun)
- `gold_spread` 0.0035 (rifle 0.007), `gold_aim_spread` 0.001 (rifle 0.002), `gold_aim_fov` 45, `gold_aim_speed` 0.6, `gold_recoil` 0.016 (rifle 0.022, fixed in code): the Rifle dorado's accuracy. Its reach is the same hitscan ray as every human gun (no range key: a bot's `range` never applies, bots never get it)
- `sfx_gold_level`, `sfx_gold_tail`, `fx_gold_flash`, `fx_gold_tracer_w`, `fx_gold_tracer_life`: the Rifle dorado's shot sound (the rifle's sound design) and flash and tracer sizes
- `gold_color` 0xffc400, `gold_dark_color` 0xb27e00, `gold_tracer_color` 0xffe280 (as decimal numbers): the Rifle dorado's body, its darker parts and its tracer, in the hand and on the model
- `hunter_color` 0xff6666 (decimal): the enemy marks on the minimap and the bar while the Cazador runs

## Story 019: Free for All mode (option "Modo de juego: Bomba / Free for All", Bomba by default)
- `ffa_duration` 300: seconds a Free for All match lasts (one timed match, no rounds); the most kills wins, a tie goes to fewer deaths
- `ffa_wrong_wait` 4: seconds a wrong answer to the respawn question waits before another question (fixed, it does not grow like wrong_delay)
- `ffa_bot_respawn` 6: seconds a dead bot waits before it comes back (no question)
- `ffa_kill_money` 300 (kill_money in Bomba is 300): money per kill in Free for All; there is no round pay and no first-kill bonus
- `ffa_spawn_dist` 30: metres a respawn point should be from every living enemy (one is drawn at random among those that far; when none is, the farthest); story 021: the points are every walkable cell of the whole map (the first floor too, ramps left out), at the start of the match and at each respawn
- `ffa_buy_menu_time` 10: seconds the buy menu stays open after a respawn and at the start (a buy question open holds it; the player can close it before). Story 021: the same seconds are how long that person stays invisible and untouchable (a hard clock: an open question does not stretch it); a shop opened later with the buy key (B) or the touch button uses the same time to close by itself but hides nobody
- `ffa_bot_skill` 0.5: the bots' skill ramp (0 = bot_acc_start/bot_react_start, 1 = the _end values) in Free for All, where there are no rounds to ramp over; the bot level's multipliers apply on top
- `duel_duration` 300: seconds a Duelo por equipos match lasts (one timed match, no rounds, bomb, sites or halftime); the team with more kills wins, equal kills is "Empate"
- `duel_bot_respawn` 6: seconds a dead bot waits before it comes back in Duelo por equipos (no question); a person answers the respawn question (the wrong-answer wait is `ffa_wrong_wait`)
- `duel_kill_money` 300: money per kill in Duelo por equipos; there is no round pay and no first-kill bonus (a teammate's kill by friendly fire pays nothing)
- `duel_rival_dist` 25: metres a respawn point should be from every living rival (one is drawn at random among those that far; when none is, the farthest)
- `duel_mate_dist` 12: metres from the nearest living teammate within which a respawn point is looked for (with no living teammate any point of the map may be drawn)
- `duel_mate_gap` 2: metres a respawn point keeps from every living teammate, so nobody lands on top of another
- `duel_bot_skill` 0.5: the bots' skill ramp (0 = bot_acc_start/bot_react_start, 1 = the _end values) in Duelo por equipos; the bot level's multipliers apply on top

## Story 025: Oleadas cooperativas (option "Modo de juego", mode id `waves`)
- `waves_start_count` 3: enemy bots in wave 1; every wave adds `waves_growth` more (wave n has start + growth x (n - 1)), up to `waves_max_count`. Only the roster's four enemy seats exist, so at most 4 enemies are on the map at once; the rest of the wave comes in as the first ones fall
- `waves_growth` 2: enemy bots each wave adds over the one before
- `waves_max_count` 16: the cap on the bots in one wave (the waves go on at this size, with the skill still rising up to `waves_skill_max`)
- `waves_skill_start` 0.2, `waves_skill_step` 0.1, `waves_skill_max` 1: the enemy bots' level in wave n is start + step x (n - 1), at most max (the same 0..1 ramp as `ffa_bot_skill`: 0 = bot_acc_start/bot_react_start, 1 = the _end values; the bot level's multiplier applies on top)
- `waves_mate_skill` 0.5: the skill ramp of the teammate bots (players' side), fixed so they do not grow stronger with the waves
- `waves_smg_from` 3, `waves_rifle_from` 6: the first wave in which enemy bots carry a Subfusil, and a Rifle (before that the pistol; the rifle wins from its wave on)
- `waves_pause` 20: seconds of pause before wave 1 and after each wave is cleared, with the shop open (a dead player answers the respawn question in it and comes back when the next wave starts)
- `waves_spawn_gap` 2: seconds between one enemy coming in and the next while a seat is free
- `waves_enemy_spawn_dist` 25: metres an enemy's spawn point should be from every living player (one is drawn at random among those that far; when none is, the farthest); the points are the same cells as Free for All's
- `waves_hunt_interval` 4: seconds between the orders that send an enemy bot with nothing in sight toward the nearest player standing, and a teammate bot toward the nearest enemy
- `waves_notice_time` 3: seconds the big "Oleada N" notice stays on screen when a wave starts
- `waves_kill_money` 300: money per kill in Oleadas cooperativas; there is no round pay, wave pay or first-kill bonus. The wrong-answer wait of the respawn question is `ffa_wrong_wait`
