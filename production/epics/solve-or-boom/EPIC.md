# Epic: Solve or Boom

> **Source**: `design/game-brief.md` (the minimal-tier design record)
> **Goal**: A team bomb shooter where every gun, every ammo clip and every defuse costs one right answer.

## Scope

The brief's Features list. All 9 items were built by the first build and iteration 1, and are marked `[x]`. What is left is deepening the one thin stand-in system from Your systems.

Build order from the brief:
1. The slice: one full match on one map. Done.
2. Grow the built-in question banks per subject and content. This is story 001.
3. Feel pass: gun sounds, hit feedback, smarter bot routes. No story: nothing is unbuilt in Features. Add it to the brief's Features to get one.
